Features

Image detection

Upload any image and get bounding boxes, class labels, and confidence scores instantly.

Live webcam

Run detection on your own camera feed in real time, streamed over WebSockets.

DroidCam phone

Point your Android/iOS phone camera at the scene and detect over WiFi.

80 COCO classes

3-scale detection at 13×13 / 26×26 / 52×52 with CUDA auto-detection and CPU fallback.

How it works

  1. Upload / stream — send an image, webcam frames, or a DroidCam MJPEG stream.
  2. YOLOv3 inference — the Darknet-53 model decodes 3 scales and applies non-max suppression.
  3. Annotate — boxes, labels, and confidences are drawn and returned to your browser.
